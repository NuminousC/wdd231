Site Plan: The Beginner Developer Glossary

Site Name

The Beginner Developer Glossary

This name directly describes what the site does and who it is for — a glossary built specifically for people, including myself, who are new to web development and need plain-language explanations of the terms they encounter while learning.

Site Purpose

The site provides a searchable, filterable glossary of core web-development terms covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and general web fundamentals. Each term includes a plain-language definition, a short example, a category, and a difficulty level. The site also includes a beginner learning-path guide that shows how the glossary's categories fit together, along with a form for visitors to suggest new terms to add.

Scenarios

  • I keep hearing terms like “DOM” and “API” in my course lectures — what do they actually mean?
  • I want a quick definition and example without digging through a whole textbook chapter — where can I find that fast?
  • I found a term I don’t understand that isn’t in the glossary — how do I suggest it be added?

Color Scheme

The palette is inspired by code editors and developer tools, to give the glossary a focused, technical feel appropriate to its subject matter.

  • Deep Navy — #1B2A41

    Headings, header/footer background, nav text

  • Terminal Green — #3DDC97

    Buttons, links, active states, and accent borders (used on dark or white backgrounds only, never as body text)

  • Near White — #F7F9FC

    Page background

  • Dark Slate — #1F2937

    Body text
